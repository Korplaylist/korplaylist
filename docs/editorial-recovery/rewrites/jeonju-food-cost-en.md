Planning a Jeonju Food Budget: Meals, Shared Snacks and Menu Checks
Calculate bibimbap, bean-sprout soup and market snacks separately, avoid outdated branch prices, and use a clearly labelled two-person budget example.

Build a Jeonju food budget around **two meals plus shared extras**, not one supposedly universal daily average. The numbers below are hypothetical planning inputs, not current restaurant quotations or receipts from a personally tested food tour.

## Match the Price to the Branch

Jeonju's official food map combines restaurant locations, dishes and prices, but its publication date matters. An official PDF is not proof that an old price still applies. Use it to identify an establishment, then verify the exact branch's current menu before ordering. [Jeonju city food map](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/images/file/map/02_mapFood_kor.pdf)

A Jeonju main branch, a Nambu Market branch and a department-store outlet in another city are different locations. A search result with the same restaurant name may show the wrong menu. This guide does not fill that gap with an invented “current” price.

## A Two-Person Calculation

Every unit price in this table is an **illustrative assumption to replace with your own menu prices**.

| Order | Assumed unit price | Quantity | Subtotal |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| First meal | KRW 9,000 | 2 | KRW 18,000 |
| Second meal | KRW 15,000 | 2 | KRW 30,000 |
| Shared market snack | KRW 6,000 | 1 | KRW 6,000 |
| Cafe drinks | KRW 5,000 | 2 | KRW 10,000 |
| Total | | | KRW 64,000 |

That example is KRW 32,000 per person. Replacing the second meal with an actual KRW 18,000 menu item makes the total KRW 70,000, or KRW 35,000 each. The shared snack is counted once, not multiplied by two again. Transport, accommodation and food bought as gifts are outside this calculation.

## Soup Extras and Bibimbap Formats

Hyundaiok's official menu distinguishes Nambu Market-style bean-sprout soup from boiled versions and lists squid as a separately sold addition. Do not assume an extra is included because a photograph shows it beside the soup. Availability can differ by branch. [Hyundaiok menu descriptions](https://www.hyundaiok.com/menu/01)

For bibimbap, compare the exact dish, not only the word “bibimbap”: a single dish and a set meal can include different items. Write down the menu name, portion count and extras. If you have a full bibimbap lunch, sharing one snack at the market can make more sense than scheduling another large meal immediately afterwards.

## Put Market Shopping in the Right Part of the Walk

The city's guide describes Nambu Market and Pungnammun as close to the Hanok Village area. A normal market shop and a night-market stall are not the same operating promise, however. Check the dated night-market notice if a specific stall is your reason for visiting. [Jeonju's Nambu Market guide](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/index.jeonju?menuCd=DOM_000000112001002000)

Before the trip, make one line per order: establishment, branch, dish, unit price and quantity. Apply minimum-order rules before splitting a bill among the group. If the total exceeds your budget, protect the meal you most want and reduce optional drinks or takeaway quantities.

References checked September 13, 2026. Current branch prices have not been independently verified; this is a budgeting method, not a live price list.
