First Three Days in Jeju: Choose an East or West Coast Base
Turn flight times into usable sightseeing time and build a two-night stay around one district rather than an island-wide checklist.

Three calendar days do not mean three full sightseeing days. With a late arrival and early departure, only the middle day may be long. Calculate **arrival at the hotel and departure from it**, not just the flight duration, before choosing an east or west coast base.

## Choose a District by the Main Experience

Compare the east if Seongsan and nearby coastal villages are the purpose; compare the west if time around Aewol and Hallim matters more. This is a choice of movement, not a ranking of the coasts. Select two or three actual places using [Visit Jeju's official information](https://www.visitjeju.net/kr/main/index.do) before adding secondary stops.

| Day | Main job | Addition to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | Reach the base and eat nearby | Crossing to the opposite coast after a late flight |
| Middle day | Main attraction plus a nearby stop | Trying to cover east and west together |
| Departure | Retrieve bags and reach the airport | A ferry outing or lengthy remote hike |

## A Bus Plan Is Not a Driving Plan

For buses, check actual stops and the next departure between attractions. The official system identifies 201 with the eastern corridor and 202 with the western one, but that does not place a stop at every hotel. [Jeju official schedules](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/publicTrafficInformation/generalBusSchedule)

A rental car also requires collection, return and parking time. If you add an island visit such as Udo, treat boarding, the return sailing and possible disruption as a separate activity block. Having a rental car does not establish permission to take it on every island ferry.

## One Hotel or Two?

Staying two nights in one district reduces check-out gaps and luggage handling, particularly on a first bus-based trip. Changing hotels makes more sense when it continues a clear route rather than chasing a different room view. Include the final airport transfer in that comparison.

For bad weather, choose a confirmed indoor alternative in the same district. Crossing the island merely to find a substitute may create more transport work than the activity is worth.
