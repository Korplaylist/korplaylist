Daegu Modern Alleys: Cheongna Hill, Gyesan Cathedral and Jingolmok
Use Daegu's official Route 2 as a walkable core, distinguish building visits from open streets, and decide whether Kim Gwangseok Street belongs in a separate outing.

Start with **Modern Culture Alley Route 2**, not a list of loosely connected Daegu attractions. The city describes this route as approximately **1.64 km and two hours**. That is the official course estimate, not a personally timed walk with lunch and every museum included. ## Start Above the City Streets

Beginning at Cheongna Hill puts the missionary houses and the March 1 movement steps before the cathedral and lower streets. It helps you read the relationship between the hillside and the city instead of retracing an arbitrary sequence of map pins. The city's walking directions describe access from **Seomun Market Station Exit 4**, via Dalgubeol-daero 405-gil toward the missionary museum area. ## An Open Street Is Not an Open Museum

Walking access does not prove that a historic house, museum or church interior is available at the same time. Choose the one interior that matters most, then check its own calendar before fixing the route. At Gyesan Cathedral, worship takes precedence over sightseeing. Avoid treating every building as a photo stop with unrestricted admission.

On a wet day, an open exhibition around Yangnyeongsi plus a meal can replace part of the outdoor circuit. Confirm the exhibition is actually operating that day; a museum name alone is not a rain plan. If steps are unsuitable, begin with the lower streets and accept that this is a shortened visit rather than describing the full hill route as step-free.

## Keep Kim Gwangseok Street Separate

Kim Gwangseok Street is not simply the next alley at the end of official Route 2. Add a separate transfer and visit block if you want it. For an architecture-led day, spend that time inside one historic attraction instead. For music and murals, shorten the modern-alley section and make the second district the afternoon's main purpose.

Budget for the transport and specific interiors you choose, plus food. Do not invent a combined admission fee or call the entire outing free because the streets are accessible.
