Gimhae Airport to Gwangalli: Sasang Transfer and the Right Beach Station
Follow the light rail to Sasang and Metro Line 2, compare Gwangan with Geumnyeonsan, and plan the hotel walk, luggage and late-arrival alternatives.

For a rail journey from Gimhae Airport to Gwangalli, compare **Airport Station to Sasang by light rail, then Metro Line 2 toward Jangsan**. The important final decision is your hotel address: Gwangalli is a waterfront district, not one station exit. This guide uses operator information checked on September 13, 2026; it is not a personally timed airport transfer.

## Find the Light Rail, Then Change at Sasang

After immigration and baggage reclaim where applicable, follow signs for the Busan-Gimhae Light Rail Airport Station. Check that the train is heading toward **Sasang**, not toward Gimhae city. Time spent leaving the terminal and reaching the station is additional to the train journey. Use [Gimhae Airport's official site](https://www.airport.co.kr/gimhaeeng/index.do) for airport facilities and [the light-rail operator](https://www.bglrt.com/00163.web) for ticket information.

At Sasang, follow the Metro Line 2 transfer signs. This is a transfer between systems, not a promise of a train across the same platform. With a suitcase or mobility needs, locate the lift route before following the nearest staircase. On Line 2, choose the **Jangsan direction**.

## Gwangan or Geumnyeonsan?

Gwangan is station **209** and Geumnyeonsan is **210**. The metro operator identifies Gwangalli Beach as a destination for Gwangan Station. Still, a hotel near the southern beach may be better approached from Geumnyeonsan. [Busan Metro's Gwangan station information](https://www2.humetro.busan.kr/homepage/default/broadcast/list.do?CODE=209&SUB_CODE=2)

| Hotel location | Start by comparing | Do not overlook |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Central or northern Gwangalli waterfront | Gwangan | The actual hotel entrance, not its beach-facing facade |
| Southern beach toward Namcheon | Geumnyeonsan | Street crossings and the walk from the exit |
| Farther into the Millak waterfront area | Gwangan plus a local connection | The last stretch with luggage |

Search the hotel's full street address from both stations. A listing that says “near Gwangalli” is not enough to pick an exit. Save the Korean address as well as the hotel name, particularly if you might finish the journey by taxi.

## Tickets and the Total Journey

Do not assume that adding two standalone ticket prices reproduces a transport-card transfer fare. Equally, do not assume separate single-journey tickets receive the same transfer treatment as a supported card. The light-rail fare page announces the removal of the former regional transfer surcharge, so an old blog's additional KRW 500 should not automatically be added. Check the rules for the ticket you actually use. [Official light-rail fares](https://www.bglrt.com/00163.web)

This guide does not publish an unverified fixed total fare or a minute-perfect airport-to-hotel time. Your plan must include terminal formalities, station access, train waiting, the Sasang interchange and the final walk. A dinner reservation based only on time aboard the trains can be too tight even when the railway runs normally.

## When to Compare a Taxi

Several large bags, limited mobility or a hotel well beyond the useful metro exits can justify comparing a taxi. Compare the live quote **to the hotel door**, divided among the passengers, against the full rail journey rather than only the metro ride. Check that one vehicle can accommodate the entire group and its luggage.

For a late arrival, confirm both the last light-rail departure you can reach and the onward Line 2 connection at Sasang. Being able to board the first train does not prove that the whole journey is still possible. If the connection is uncertain, consult the official airport taxi arrangements and notify your hotel about late check-in.

Once checked in, a nearby meal and a short Gwangalli walk make a manageable first evening. Going back across Busan to add another waterfront area repeats the travel you have just completed; keep Haeundae or the old city for a separate outing.
