Yeosu Island Day Trip: Odongdo or Geumodo, and Which Ferry Port?
Separate causeway access from a ferry-and-trail day, distinguish Singi, Yeocheon and Hamgumi, and plan the island's return connection.

An island outing in Yeosu can mean two very different days. Odongdo has a causeway connection; Geumodo requires a ferry and transport on the island. Decide whether you want a short coastal walk or a full ferry-and-trail outing before searching tickets.

## Odongdo Does Not Require a Ferry First

The city describes a 768-metre breakwater approach with walking access. That does not mean every path within the island is flat. Separate reaching the entrance from exploring the woodland, and include the return walk in a short visit. [Yeosu's Odongdo information](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/10tour/odongdo)

## Geumodo: Record Both Port Names

**Singi on Dolsan to Yeocheon on Geumodo** is not the same departure as **Yeosu Passenger Terminal to Hamgumi**. The mainland approach and the connection to your chosen trail differ. Do not book solely because the destination says Geumodo. [City information on port connections](https://m.yeosu.go.kr/tour/leisure/island/ando_info)

Write four linked items: outward ferry ports and date; trail start and finish; island transport between ports and trail; return sailing and arrival time at the pier. Without the third item, a booked return ticket may still leave an unplanned journey across the island.

## Choose One Bireonggil Section

The city lists Route 1 from Hamgumi to Dupo as 5 km and about two hours. That course estimate does not include reaching the mainland terminal or waiting for the ferry. Arrange the way back from the finishing village before committing to the walk. [Official Bireonggil route information](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/leisure/walk/geumodo_bireonggil)

Prepare for a coastal trail rather than an urban cafe stroll, and follow closures. If slopes or prolonged walking are unsuitable, compare a shorter land-access visit instead of treating the whole trail network as the default day trip.

## Protect the Return

The city notes identification requirements and weather-related disruption. For Singi–Yeocheon, check the date and direction in the [route's sailing and booking information](https://www.geumodoferry.com/index.php). A successful morning sailing does not guarantee the afternoon return. Avoid a tight connection to the final onward train or bus out of Yeosu.
