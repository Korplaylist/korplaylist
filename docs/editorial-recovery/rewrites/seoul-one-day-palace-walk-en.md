One Palace Day in Seoul: Gyeongbokgung and Either Seochon or Insadong
Use the correct daytime opening window, distinguish guided and special visits, and choose one neighbouring district instead of collecting palaces.

For a first palace day, make **Gyeongbokgung the main visit and choose either Seochon or Insadong afterwards**. This leaves time to read the palace's interpretation and eat without turning the day into five abbreviated attractions. It is a self-guided planning proposal, not a packaged tour or a first-hand visit report.

## Put Admission Before Lunch Reservations

For September and October, the official daytime schedule lists Gyeongbokgung as **09:00–18:00, with last admission at 17:00**. That final entry is not a recommended arrival time for a complete visit. Check the travel date's closure notices as well; Tuesday is the regular closed day, with holiday arrangements requiring separate confirmation. [Royal Palaces and Tombs Center opening information](https://royal.cha.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R702000000.do/)

Arrive with enough room for ticketing and the parts of the grounds you want to see. Allowing two to three hours is an editorial allocation, not the duration of an official guided tour. If you have only an hour, select a smaller part of the grounds rather than claiming to have followed a full palace route.

## Three Different Kinds of Visit

| Visit type | Check | Do not assume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary daytime entry | Opening date, entry conditions and current fee | Every pavilion interior is included |
| Regular guided interpretation | Language, meeting place and participation rules | An English tour runs whenever you arrive |
| A special programme or evening event | Separate date, reservation and admission notice | A daytime ticket covers it |

Use the [official Gyeongbokgung interpretation page](https://royal.cha.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R706010000.do) for language-specific details. If the tour is your priority, arrange the meal around that meeting time. If independent walking matters more, do not let a missed tour cause a rushed or confusing visit.

## Choose One Neighbourhood Afterwards

Choose **Seochon** for a west-side meal and a limited neighbourhood walk. Choose **Insadong** for an east/southeast-side continuation focused on shops, galleries or tea. Save your chosen destination before leaving the palace so you use an exit that makes sense. This is not a recommendation to walk through private residential courtyards or photograph people at home.

Do not add both neighbourhoods, Bukchon and another palace as mandatory stops simply because their names appear together in travel searches. A second paid site has its own closing time and may need a separate reservation. If you want another heritage visit, replace the neighbourhood block and check that site's rules instead.

## Weather and Clothing Choices

The palace is substantially an outdoor visit. In heat, keep an indoor meal or rest block after it; in persistent rain, shorten exposed walking. Hanbok rental is optional and adds fitting, payment, storage and return time. Verify the palace's current free-admission clothing criteria separately from a rental shop's advertising before relying on an exemption.

For an evening outside the palace area, choose a separate [Hangang park plan](/en/travel/seoul/seoul-hangang-evening/) only when the transfer and weather still suit the day.
