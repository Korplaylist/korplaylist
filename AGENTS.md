# Future Article Writing

Before planning, drafting or reviewing articles, read `.automation/manual-editorial-policy.json` and `docs/article-writing-prompt.md`. These are the authoritative writing instructions for Korean, English and Japanese; conflicting historical queue prompts and rebuild templates are not current guidance.

Existing articles, frontmatter, URLs and images are protected. Do not rewrite, remove, replace, hide or redate them unless the user explicitly authorizes that separate work. Never run bulk content/image repair or rebuild scripts for a new-article request.

Keep article automation paused. A writing request does not authorize enabling recurring jobs. Do not change unrelated worktree files.
