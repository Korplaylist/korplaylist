For a first visit to Busan's Songdo cable car, a standard round-trip ticket is the straightforward choice. It takes you across the bay and back without requiring a separate return route. Upgrade to a glass-floor cabin only if looking down through the floor is part of the experience you actually want; the general coastal view is not exclusive to the more expensive option.

This guide covers Busan Air Cruise at Songdo Beach in western Busan. It is not Songdo Central Park in Incheon, and it is not the Sky Capsule at Haeundae. Save the operator's name and the correct station before buying tickets.

## Check live operation before buying

Start with the [Busan Air Cruise official homepage](https://busanaircruise.co.kr/main/main.html), not just a general tourism listing. The operator displays the day's status. During our September 10, 2026 check, it showed a weather-related closure notice; that dated observation is not a forecast for your visit. Always refresh the current notice before leaving your accommodation.

The [official seasonal timetable](https://busanaircruise.co.kr/info/time?rank=2) lists 09:00 to 20:00 in December through February, 09:00 to 21:00 in March through June and September through November, and 09:00 to 22:00 in July and August. Ticket sales normally end 30 minutes before closing. Weather and operational conditions can change those arrangements.

A roofed cabin does not make the cable car a guaranteed rainy-day activity. Wind can affect operation, and low visibility can remove much of the reason to pay for the ride. Keep a flexible Nampo-area alternative rather than crossing the city on the assumption that a purchased voucher guarantees service.

## Current tickets: Air Cruise or Crystal Cruise?

Use the [operator's fare table](https://busanaircruise.co.kr/info/price?rank=1) as the primary price reference. At our check, the listed adult fares were:

| Ticket | Round trip | One way |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Air Cruise, standard floor | 19,000 won | 15,000 won |
| Crystal Cruise, glass floor | 24,000 won | 19,000 won |

These are the displayed standard adult fares, not a promise about a future promotion. Child categories and discounts have separate conditions. Check the age definition and any required proof before paying for a family.

For two adults, the listed round-trip total is 38,000 won in standard cabins or 48,000 won in glass-floor cabins, before transport, food or other attractions. The 10,000-won difference is worth considering as part of the whole outing: someone who dislikes looking down may enjoy the cheaper cabin more.

A round-trip ticket does not mean you remain in the cabin for a continuous loop. The [operator's boarding rules](https://busanaircruise.co.kr/info/use?rank=3) require passengers to get off and board again for the return. Keep the ticket until both rides are complete. Ordinary cabins may be shared with other passengers; a normal ticket is not a private reservation.

One-way tickets need a deliberate onward plan and are valid from the issuing station under the operator's rules. Do not choose one only because it looks cheaper, then discover that you still need to arrange a road journey back. For most first-time visitors starting at the beach, round trip keeps the route simpler.

## Get to Songdo Base Station

The beach-side departure point is Songdo Base Station at 171 Songdohaebyeon-ro, Seo-gu. The [official access page](https://busanaircruise.co.kr/info/location?rank=5) lists bus connections from Nampo or Jagalchi and a bus 26 option from Busan Station toward Amnam-dong Community Service Center, followed by a walk of about 10 minutes.

Use those as starting points for a live journey search. Confirm the boarding stop and direction on the day; different station exits can lead to different bus stops. Do not treat an old exit number in a blog as more reliable than current station signs or the operator's directions.

A taxi can simplify the final leg for a group, but road traffic still matters. Show the full station name rather than only "Songdo." If you are driving, check which parking lot's terms apply: the operator distinguishes station-area parking from the separate public lot at Amnam Park.

Leave large luggage at your accommodation when possible. The outing includes queues, boarding, walking and possibly steps at viewpoints. A bag that fits in a taxi can still make the visitor route uncomfortable.

## What to do after crossing the bay

The cable car connects the beach side with Songdo Sky Park near Amnam Park. [Visit Busan's attraction introduction](https://www.visitbusan.net/kr/index.do?lang_cd=en&menuCd=DOM_000000301001001000&uc_seq=1422) describes the approximately 1.62-kilometer crossing and nearby park options. Its general fare summary may lag behind the operator, which is why the price section above uses the direct fare table.

Spend time at the arrival side before joining the return queue. A short look at the views and a rest may be enough; you do not need to purchase every extra experience. Decide how much walking is comfortable before setting off into the park.

Amnam Park, Songdo Yonggung Suspension Bridge and the beach-side skywalk are not interchangeable names for the cable car. Separate facilities can have their own opening times, fees and weather restrictions. Do not assume your cable-car ticket includes bridge admission or that a coastal path must be open because the cable car is running.

For a family or someone with limited walking stamina, a compact station-area visit may be better than an ambitious park loop. Mobility-aid users should check the operator's current boarding requirements directly, including equipment dimensions and assistance. Avoid relying on a broad claim that every cabin and onward path is universally accessible.

## A flexible half-day from Nampo

Allow roughly 30 to 60 minutes for getting to the beach side from the Nampo area, depending on waiting and traffic, then leave a generous block for ticketing and boarding. Plan another 45 to 75 minutes for the far-side views and a short break before the return. These are our planning allowances, not guaranteed transport or queue times.

After returning, take a short beach promenade walk if conditions are pleasant, then head back toward your meal or hotel. Keep three to four hours for the overall outing if you prefer not to rush. A busy holiday can require more time, while a quiet day with no park detour can be shorter.

Daylight is the easier first-visit choice because you can see the coastline clearly and judge the walking route. If sunset is your priority, check that day's sunset and allow for both boarding queues. Buying the final available departure is not a reliable way to guarantee a particular light condition.

Do not schedule the cable car immediately before a KTX departure or a distant dinner reservation. Your return time depends on more than the duration of the ride. Keep your next commitment flexible or move this outing to a different day.

## What to do if it is closed

If the operator has suspended service, do not wait indefinitely without a reopening estimate. Ask how an unused ticket or voucher is handled through the channel where you bought it. Operator tickets and third-party vouchers may have different procedures; keep the purchase confirmation.

For a western-Busan alternative, use our [Busan market food route](/en/travel/busan/busan-market-food/) and choose a seated meal or covered shopping area. For a different coastal transport experience on another day, compare the [Haeundae Beach Train and Sky Capsule](/en/travel/busan/busan-blueline-beach-train-vs-sky-capsule/). They are not nearby substitutes to reach casually when Songdo stops running.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the glass-floor ticket necessary?

No. Standard cabins provide the coastal crossing. Choose glass floors only when that extra downward view appeals to everyone using the ticket.

### Can I stay in the cabin for the return?

No. The operator requires round-trip passengers to disembark and board again. Retain your ticket for the return check.

### Is Songdo cable car in Incheon?

This attraction is in Busan's Seo-gu, beside Songdo Beach. Incheon's Songdo is a separate destination.
