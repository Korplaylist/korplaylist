Anchor a Hanok Village day around Gyeonggijeon, then place a lane walk and market meal around it. The neighborhood is not one ticketed theme park. **Shrine admission, a guided walk and an individual craft activity are separate decisions.**

## Gyeonggijeon Hours and Admission

The official village guide lists 09:00–19:00 for March–May and September–October, 09:00–20:00 for June–August, and 09:00–18:00 for November–February. Standard tickets are KRW 3,000 for adults, 2,000 for youths and military visitors, and 1,000 for children. Do not confuse resident discounts with the standard ticket. Check last admission separately. [Official heritage information](https://hanok.jeonju.go.kr/contents/place)

## Choose the Tour Before Lunch

The official schedule places the Gyeonggijeon tour's meeting point inside the main gate at Hongsalmun. The lane tour uses a different meeting point, such as the ticket office. Foreign-language options are listed for the 11:00 and 14:00 Gyeonggijeon sessions; confirm your language and day's operation. Admission is separate from guide arrangements. [Official guided-tour schedule](https://hanok.jeonju.go.kr/contents/ch_tour)

For an 11:00 tour, complete arrival formalities and ticket purchase beforehand rather than booking lunch at the same time. Afterwards, choose one further building that relates to what interested you. This gives the afternoon a purpose beyond collecting photographs of every doorway.

## Two Afternoon Options

| Your interest | Route emphasis | Adjustment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Architecture and quieter lanes | Extend toward the Hyanggyo area | Shorten walking if adding hanbok rental or a cafe |
| Food and market shopping | Pungnammun, then Nambu Market | Buy takeaway items near the end |

The city describes Nambu Market as close to the village district. A regular market shop is not a promise that a particular night-market stall will operate on your date. [Jeonju's Nambu Market guide](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/index.jeonju?menuCd=DOM_000000112001002000)

If renting hanbok, include the return to the rental shop in your route. Shopping first and then doubling back with bags is less convenient. At Jeondong Cathedral, respect worship and access notices rather than treating the church as an unrestricted exhibition.

## A Shortened Wet-Day Visit

Keep Gyeonggijeon and one meal, then choose a single confirmed indoor activity if time remains. Verify craft-workshop availability before relying on it as the rain alternative. A statement that the village is accessible around the clock does not mean its buildings remain open all night.
